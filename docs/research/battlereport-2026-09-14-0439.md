**The sequence is not — and the reason is this report's bonus fit, not the model.** The first version of this

gives only to categorised units. Fitted the right way round (base +157 / +187, the buff on categories only),
the replay improves from **22 mismatches to 9**, the **first fourteen entries pair exactly**, ten of the 24
damage lines are identical to the unit, and the game's realised total falls **inside** the engine's band:

| the game realised, proc halved | 1,575,922 |
| the engine, same march and fit — min / avg / max | 1,536,421 / 1,571,711 / 1,607,001 |

The nine remaining entries are one knife-edge, and it is the app's: the engine rounds `hpPerUnit` to an
integer where the game carries it unrounded (audit E5), and that 0.03 % is enough to swap **Archer I
(155,310) and Rider II (155,244) — two stacks 0.04 % apart** — after which every downstream line shifts.
Nothing before the swap is wrong, which is why the first fourteen entries land exactly.

**The lesson.** This ladder packs its rungs 0.2–1 % apart in HP, which is what maximises the number of
stacks, and it is therefore a coin flip away from a different strike assignment. A minimum step of 2–3 %
between consecutive stacks should make a plan robust to a 1 % stat error at a small cost in rung count.

## Unresolved

- **The EMH VI "shortfall" is resolved — it was the buff.** The category-less mercenary sits two points below
  the troops in both reports because the owner's +2 health / +1 strength buff reaches only units that carry a
  category, and the first fit of this fight put those two points on the **guardsmen** key instead. With the
  buff as its own source the unit's line reproduces exactly (`10 × 6,090 × 2.5701 = 156,513`), and the app's
  table needs no change — only the profile does.
- **The residual nine entries are the app's HP rounding**, not a rule: `hpPerUnit` is rounded to an integer
  where the game keeps the fraction (audit E5), worth 0.03 %, which flips Archer I against Rider II because
  this plan's ladder packs them 0.04 % apart.
- **Why the bonuses moved by two points** between 00:41 and 04:39 the same morning: a temporary
  buff expiring, a gear change, or a different captain. The report cannot say.
- **The enemy's per-unit HP** (the giant's ~19.7 k a unit, the fire woman's ~70–150 k) is inferred from the
  badges and the figure deltas; the app does not model it, and the monster's *raw* damage is still
  unknowable from a journal whose enemy line is the stack's HP.
