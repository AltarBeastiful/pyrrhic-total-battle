
## The frame

Owner's export, scenario C, 4 343 leadership / 2 000 authority, horizon **4**, no budget, the bar capped at 4 stops. Stock: EMH 92 · arbalester 76 · legionary 72 · chariot 37.

Three ways of drawing the bar (*current* = S-59 as shipped, sorted by campaign silver; *current + merge* = the same with stops that burn the same and sit within 2 % of each other on damage and silver a march merged; *burn axis* = one plan a burn level, thriftiest first, the same sweet spot), crossed with the S-58 flags (A = the grid's thrift end samples one chunk of every hired type, B = the band refuses a plan that fields none of a stocked type). A changes the search; B and the bar's way change only what is offered, so the rows with A off share one search.

A row is the **repeated march**; "campaign" columns are the plan's 4 marches with the finale.
