## What the data says the definition is

- **Unlimited silver**: the most campaign damage is **46,118,086** from **O2 · the best campaign** (the plan's recommendation). The one-march optimum, O1, gets 37,958,896 — 82.3 % of it.
- **Under the purse**: the most damage is **24,854,746** from **O3 · the best damage a silver**. O1 gets 14,205,005.
- **The ratio ends are not the answer.** O4 (best damage a mercenary) spends 21,613,000 silver for 16,851,860 damage, and O3 (best damage a silver) spends 4,158,000 for 24,854,746. A ratio names a *direction*, never a stopping point.
