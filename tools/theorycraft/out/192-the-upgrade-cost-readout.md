# 192 — the upgrade cost readout

The advisor's default pass (`runAdvisor` over the 29 probes of `genericProbes()`: +1 point on each of 13 health and 13 strength lines, +1 % of the leadership, authority and dominance pools) on 20 armies: the owner's account of 2026-10-07 (`pyrrhic-my-account-2026-10-07.json`, read only, planned as the app plans it: `buildPlanRequest` on its active setup) and the benchmark's 19. One lane in Node, no clock in any job (the 20 000-dominance camp alone under the pass's 20 s clock), every march read as the March shows it (Tight). Each row is read on the **sweet spot**, the stop the card headlines by default, and its march cost is the card's own: `costChange` (the bill of the reading the gain is, re-planned or re-priced, minus the current march's: silver, gold and training seconds, raw amounts per march) and `outstandingSeconds` under the shipped bound `CAMPAIGN.outstandingTraining` (10 % of the current queue **and** 1h 0m, both strictly exceeded), printed with the card's notation (`compactTwo`, `duration`). Display only: no rating, ranking or constant was changed.

Armies read: 19 of 20.

## Reading (measured figures only)

Written by hand from this run's figures (the script does not write these two sections: a re-run rewrites the file without them).

**What the line prints.** On the top 10 of the 19 armies read, 153 rows gain and the card prints a march-cost line under 69 of them (45.1 %): silver on all 69, gold on 16 (10.5 %), the training time on 3 (2.0 %); 18 lines (11.8 %) carry a saving. The other 84 (54.9 %) print nothing because **their bill does not move at all**: no row lost its line to rounding (0 under what the card prints). By family, over all 170 gaining sweet-spot rows: **strength** leaves the bill exactly as it is on 97 of 105 (the same counts deal more damage); **health** moves it on 48 of 48 (silver −1.7 % … +1.3 % of the march, median +0.0 %); **housing** on 16 of 17: `housing:leadership` (+1 % of the pool) costs +0.6 % to +1.1 % of the march's silver, and about as much of its training queue, on the 14 armies where its silver rises (+19K to +81K a march; it is first on 9 of them, each time "costs +23K … +81K silver").

**On his account of 2026-10-07** (sweet spot: 28,139,581 damage for 3,002,754 silver, 6,391 gold, 9d 13h of training, 10 mercs lost): the four best rows are health lines (specialist, mounted, ranged, melee), each **+1.7 % damage for +119 gold** (+1.9 % of the march's gold), a few hundred silver either way (−507 … +243, 0.0 % of the march) and 2 to 20 minutes of training; the card reads "costs +119 gold; saves 279 silver" on the first. The fifth, `health:elemental`, gains on the rating while its damage falls (−0.204 %): it saves 8,400 silver, 1h 24m and 120 dragon coins, and the line says "saves 8.4K silver". The five strength rows under them (+0.061 % to +0.209 % damage) leave the bill as it is and print no line. No row on his sweet spot prints the time (0 of 16; over every stop, 3 of 80). His pass took 19.4 s on one lane.

**The training-time bound (10 % of the queue and 1 h) prints the time on few rows, not most**: 3 of 153 top-10 rows (2.0 %), 3 of 170 gaining sweet-spot rows (1.8 %), 30 of 605 gaining readings over every stop (5.0 %; 8 rises, 22 savings). The three on a sweet spot are all savings on one army (his TotalStack profile of 2026-09-19: −1d 4h, −1d 4h and −1d 3h, 18.2 % to 18.9 % of a 6d 5h queue). **The share decides, not the floor**: the 1 h floor alone passes 36 of 153 (23.5 %), the 10 % share alone 3 (2.0 %); with the share at 10 %, every floor from 1 h to 1 d prints the same 3 on the top 10, and with the floor at 1 h a 5 % share prints the same 3 (32 instead of 30 over every stop). Half the gaining rows do not move the queue at all (median 0.0 %); the 75th percentile is 0.2 % of the queue (0.9 h), the 90th 1.0 % (4.7 h), the largest 18.9 % (28.3 h). **No tighter bound is proposed**, since the bound does not print the time on most rows. Should the owner want the time more often, the looser bounds measured print it on 12 of 153 top-10 rows (7.8 %) at 2 % and 1 h, and on 18 of 153 (11.8 %) at 1 % and 1 h.

**Silver and gold have no such bound**: they are printed whenever the change is one unit or more. 35 of the 72 printed silver figures (48.6 %) are under 0.1 % of the march's silver ("saves 279 silver" on his 3.0M march), 54 (75.0 %) under 1 %, none at 10 % or more. Every printed gold figure is at least 1 % of the march's gold, and 11 of 16 are 10 % or more (+280 gold is +48.6 % of the march on the 2026-09-17 export at 7 000).

**Is the best gain the costliest?** Often, not always. The first row is the costliest in silver on **10 of the 18** armies where a top-10 row raises silver (9 with `housing:leadership` first, and the evening live account, where `health:melee` costs +54K silver and +248 gold for +15.080 % damage); in gold on **3 of the 3** where a top-10 row raises gold (his account of 2026-10-07, the four health lines tied at +119; the 2026-09-17 export at 7 000, `health:melee` +280 for +27.562 % damage; the evening live account, +248); in training time on 12 of 18; **in silver or gold on 12 of 19**. **What the line leaves out.** The rating weighs five costs and the line prints three. 12 of the 170 gaining sweet-spot rows (7.1 %) gain on the rating while their damage falls (9 on his TotalStack profile, down to −10.806 %; his account's `health:elemental`, his usual setup's `housing:dominance`, the 4 000-leadership case's `strength:ranged`): on all 12 the line reads a saving, so the trade the rating made is now on screen beside the damage. ## Left open (not changed)

1. **The training bound stays at 10 % and 1 h** (`CAMPAIGN.outstandingTraining`): it prints the time on 2.0 % of the top-10 rows and 5.0 % of every gaining reading, so the phase's condition for proposing a tighter one (the time on most rows) is not met. The owner judges it on the grid below; the constant is not changed here.
2. **A share bound on silver?** Half the printed silver figures are under 0.1 % of the march. Whether "saves 279 silver" beside "+1.7 % damage" earns its place (design rule 15) is the owner's call; the card follows the phase's rule (a change that shows in the recap's notation is printed).
3. **Mercs lost and dragon coins are rated but not printed** (12 and 2 of 170 rows). The phase asked for silver, gold and the time; adding them is a new decision.
4. **Ranking per march cost** (the phase's manual follow-up): 84 of the 153 top-10 gains (54.9 %) and 97 of 105 strength gains leave the bill exactly as it is, so a gain per silver or per gold has no denominator on them, and 12 gains are savings that come with a damage loss. Nothing is ranked by the march cost.
5. **Twins**: `strength:guardsmen` and `strength:army`, `health:guardsmen` and `health:army`, `strength:monster` and `strength:beast` read the same figures and print the same line on most armies (191's open point 3).
