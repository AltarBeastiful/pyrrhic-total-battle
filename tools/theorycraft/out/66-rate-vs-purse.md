## The two rate inputs his economy actually has

The silver renews every week and the irreplaceable stock never does, so a *purse* is the wrong unit for the second one: what a plan costs the long run is the irreplaceable units it burns, and what bounds a week is the silver the week brings in.

At his own 5,000,000 a week the plan is **5 marches, 10,682,970 damage, 4,938,100 silver**, burning 17 irreplaceable mercenaries — a stock of 38 lasts 2.2 weeks at that pace.

And the same plan, week after week with the EMH VI he earns back (95 a week against the 5 the week burns), is repeatable: the silver is the only thing it depends on, and that renews.
