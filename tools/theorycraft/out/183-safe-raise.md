## What the caps cost, and what they buy


- **`Best v2`, unbounded**: 10 of 45 stops beat `Best` — **+0.41 % at the smallest, +2.29 % median, +5.45 % at the best** — and 1 of those burns more stock, **1 chunk in all**.
- **`safe@Best` — never burns more than the shipped `Best`**: 9 of 45 stops beat `Best` — **+0.41 % at the smallest, +1.77 % median, +5.45 % at the best** — and 0 of those burn more stock, **-1 chunks in all**.
- **`safe@Plan` — never burns more than the plan’s own counts**: no stop gained damage over `Best`.

- **The stock the plain position spends to gain its damage**: over the 45 stops it beats `Best` on, `Best v2` burns 1 more chunks in all. A chunk is ten units of a hired type the account does not get back.
- **Damage at no extra cost**: `safe@Plan` beats **the plan's own counts** on 28 of 45 stops — **+0.57 % at the smallest, +3.85 % median, +19.59 % at the best** — without one extra chunk.
- **Damage a mercenary as the objective**: maximising `hiredDamage / mercLost` costs **7.25 % median damage** against `Best v2` (48.26 % at the worst, 0.00 % at the least) on the 45 stops it answers. Investigation 0019 called the ratio a trap and this is what the trap costs when it is the whole objective.
- **`burnOf` against the plan's own `mercLost`**: identical on every stop of every army.
