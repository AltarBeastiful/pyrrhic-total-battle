## The extrema of the two ratios, over the whole curve

- **most damage a silver**: 2.58 at 1,776,600 silver (4,584,601 damage, 199,330 a mercenary)
- **most damage a mercenary**: 1,148,336 at 19,198,800 silver (18,373,382 damage, 16 mercenaries, 0.96 a silver)

## Does any *single plan* beat the balanced pick on both ratios?

the balanced pick: 10,587,928 damage, 6,794,000 silver, 15 mercenaries — **1.56 a silver, 705,862 a mercenary**.
No single plan beats it on both. The two ratios trade against each other along the frontier, and this is the point where each stands as high as it can while the other does too.

## The marginal return: what the next slice of silver buys

the best step on the whole curve is 2.27 damage a silver, buying it as the plan goes from 2,144,000 to 2,482,500 silver. After about 6,400,000 silver every further slice buys less than 1 damage a silver — the curve's own answer to "how much silver".
