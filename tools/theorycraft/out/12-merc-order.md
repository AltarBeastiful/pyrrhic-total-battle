# A3 — the order among the mercenaries

The four mercenary stacks share the bottom of the kill order under M's Preservation. The one with the highest HP among them dies first, and if its kill position is ≡ 1 (mod 4) it loses a hit. Per-hit damage differs by a factor of two between them, so the order is worth money. Method: troops fixed at the sizer's `ms` ladder; for each of the 24 mercenary orders the maximum strictly descending ladder (`count = min(cap, floor(limit / hp))`, `limit` cascading); every vector checked with `feasible`, scored with `evaluateCounts`; the best refined by a ±12-unit hill climb on the mercenary counts.
