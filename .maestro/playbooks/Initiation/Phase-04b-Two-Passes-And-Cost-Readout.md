# Phase 04b: Two Compute buttons and a cost readout on every upgrade (W17 C4 follow-up)

Owner feedback of 2026-10-08, after seeing the card with typed upgrades. Two changes, in this order of value:

1. **Two buttons, two passes.** Today one Compute reads the 29 generic probes AND the typed upgrades together. The owner wants them apart: one button for "my upgrades" (the typed list only) and one for "default upgrades" (the generic probes at their default increase). Each is computed on demand, cancelled and invalidated on its own.
2. **Damage alone is not enough: show what the upgrade costs the march.** Each row shows its damage gain and the **additional cost** it brings: silver, gold, and the training time only when it is outstanding. A gain bought with a large rise in silver or gold reads very differently from a free one.

Constraints: the rules listed under "Rules for every task" in `.maestro/playbooks/Initiation/Phase-01-Worker-Pool.md` (read them first; each task runs in a fresh session): benchmark never regresses, kernel path tests, reuse, UI cites `docs/design-rules.md`, "merc" not "hired", commit per task with `Co-Authored-By: deepseek-flash <noreply@deepseek.com>`. Read also `docs/plans/advisor-card.md` and `docs/plans/progression-advisor.md` §4 C4. The cost readout is **display only**: it changes no rating, no ranking and no engine result (the rows' `gain` and the sort stay as they are); the benchmark and goldens must show zero moved reading.

**Left to decide, with the default taken here (change only if the owner says so):**

- "Additional cost" of a row = the cost of the **reading the gain is** (`StopAdvice.from`: re-planned where it is the gain, otherwise re-priced) minus the current march's, on the headline stop. Silver and gold are compared as raw amounts, per march, in the notation the recap uses.
- "Training time outstanding" = the march's `bill.seconds` changes by more than a threshold; the default threshold is 10 % of the current queue time AND at least 1 hour, held as a named constant in `src/config.ts` (not a literal in the UI). Below it the time is not printed (design rule 15).
- A cost that goes DOWN is shown too ("saves 1.2M silver"), with its sign and a word, never by colour alone.
